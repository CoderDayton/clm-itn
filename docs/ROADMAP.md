## Spanish, Portuguese and German

- Spanish "la una y diez" with nothing before "la" stays as spoken. "la" is an article, and reading it as a clock cue would also turn "la una y la otra" into "la 1 y la otra". "a la una y diez" and "es la una y diez" already work.
- Spanish and Portuguese numbers of 13 or more digits (billones, trillones, quatrilhões) stay as spoken.
- German "null uhr siebzehn" writes `null uhr 17`; a lone "null" before "uhr" has no time reading.
- Portuguese uses 1956 of its 2047 shapes, so new example coverage needs room first.
- Other languages are timed only by hand; the bench's gate and `--corpus` run English.
